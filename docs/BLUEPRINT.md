## 2. Flagged changes to your framework

These change or extend the method, so they need your approval rather than silent edits:

1. **The six frames follow your brief, not the PDF numbering.** In the PDF, structure #6 is *S + V + object + to + V*; your brief makes #6 the passive. I used the brief's six (Existence, States, Actions, Modals, Possession, Passive). *S + V + object + to + V* becomes a Day 14 "combination pattern".
2. **Perfect and perfect-continuous forms live inside Frame 3 (Actions)**, as in your brief's examples ("I have finished", "I had been waiting"). Lessons explicitly teach that *have* in "I have finished" is a helper verb, not Frame 5 possession — a common learner confusion.
3. **"The boy you said" is replaced.** It isn't grammatical English (*say* needs what was said). Used instead: "the boy you mentioned / the boy you told me about". This affects many PDF examples.
4. **Relative-pronoun rule corrected (PDF §8).** "The boy whom you say is my friend" should be "**who** you say is my friend" (he is the subject of *is*). Also: *that* can refer to people, and object pronouns can be dropped ("the phone (that) you mentioned"). *Whom* is taught as formal.
5. **Comparatives (PDF §7):** the two pattern labels were swapped with their examples (*more + adjective* was shown with "smarter"). Fixed, plus spelling rules (bigger, happier).
6. **Register labels.** *gotta, gonna, wanna* are taught as informal spoken forms only; learners write *have got to / going to / want to*.
7. **Real public figures removed** from examples (e.g., "Trump will be doing"). Names such as Tofig and Ahmad were kept alongside international names.
8. **Emotion vocabulary cleaned:** "scarry" → scary; "formidable" removed (it means impressive/intimidating, not an emotion); "to leer at" removed (it is not "laugh at" — it means looking at someone in an unpleasant sexual way); "jump/weep for joy" separated.
9. **Smaller corrections:** "There is phone" → "There is **a** phone"; "There is not reason" → "There is **no** reason"; "as expensive as pen" → "as **a** pen"; "What if I told you I am teacher" → "…I **was a** teacher"; "Phone is taken by me" → "The phone was taken by Ali" (with a note that "by me" is rarely natural); "until" removed from the perfect-tense signal words; "I get used to playing" taught mainly as "I'm getting used to / I got used to".
10. **American spelling** is used throughout (color, practice, traveled), with British differences noted; the answer checker accepts both spellings.

## 3. Learning outcomes (honest version)

After 33 days a learner who completes the lessons can: build and transform sentences in all six frames across past/present/future; ask and answer questions; use perfect, continuous and passive forms; combine clauses with conditionals, relatives and connectors; and hold short practical conversations. We do **not** promise fluency. Progress is shown as *demonstrated skills* (accuracy per frame/skill from real answers).

## 5. Architecture

- **Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind 4**, Cache Components defaults, `proxy.ts` for Supabase session refresh.
- **Content as typed code** (`src/content/days/day-XX.ts`): reviewable in git, validated by tests (every day has all 12 parts; every exercise has a correct answer; IDs unique). Lesson text has an optional `l10n` slot for future translations (e.g., Azerbaijani explanations).
- **Sentence engine** (`src/engine/`): deterministic, rule-based conjugation over a curated verb lexicon (forms + flags: transitive, state verb, natural object, passive-friendly). No generated free text; every combination either produces a tested correct sentence or an explanation of why it isn't natural.
- **Answer checker** (`src/engine/check.ts`): normalises case/punctuation/contractions; distinguishes *correct*, *also correct (alternative)*, *close — spelling*, and *incorrect with targeted feedback* for known mistakes.
- **Progress, local-first:** every learner action writes to a device store immediately (works without an account). When signed in, the same records sync to Supabase. No fabricated data — empty states until the learner acts.
- **Supabase:** Auth (email + password, magic link), Postgres with RLS. Service-role key only in server code (webhooks).
- **Entitlements:** premium lesson content is served by a server component only after a server-side subscription check. The `subscriptions` table is written only by the payment webhook (service role); users can read their own row and nothing else.
- **Audio:** browser speech synthesis (no licensing issue — uses the device's voices). Speaking: browser speech recognition where available (Chrome/Edge/Safari), clearly labelled *transcript match*, not pronunciation scoring. A `SpeechService` interface allows a real pronunciation API later.

### Retention algorithm (explainable)
Leitner boxes 1→5 with intervals 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days. Correct → next box; wrong → box 1 and due today. Every wrong exercise answer enters the review queue automatically. Daily review = due items, mistakes first, capped at a daily goal. Streaks are optional and never reset progress; the dashboard shows "days practised this week" instead of punishing gaps.

### Placement
18 questions across the six frames + combination/communication. Output: per-area result and a suggested start day. Labelled "a quick estimate, not an official CEFR test".

## 6. Design system

- **Principle:** colour means grammar. Each frame has one colour, used in every structure diagram, sentence slot and progress view. The rest of the UI is quiet cool neutrals.
- **Frame colours:** Existence teal `#0F8C82`, States indigo `#4C56D6`, Actions vermilion `#D9472B`, Modals violet `#8B4CC4`, Possession amber `#B87A06`, Passive blue `#2470B3` (with lighter dark-mode variants; all ≥4.5:1 for text use).
- **Neutrals:** paper `#F7F8FB`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#161A2E`, muted `#5B6178`, line `#E3E6EF`; dark mode uses deep indigo-ink `#131626` with lifted surfaces.
- **Type:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (UI, body — designed for legibility); Literata (example sentences, displayed as specimens).
- **Layout:** left-aligned, mobile-first; app uses a bottom tab bar on mobile and a side rail on desktop. The lesson player is distraction-free (no nav, one section at a time, progress bar).
